# PATH: Package Arrival Tracking and Handoff (SOP)

Standard operating procedure for PATH, the package tracking system: how packages that arrive for employees are checked in, how employees get notified, and how they pick packages up. PATH is also called "the new package tracking system" or "package arrival tracking".

## Overview, owner and who to ask

- **System:** PATH (Package Arrival Tracking and Handoff), a kiosk plus website plus phone check-in page with email notifications. It replaces paper sign-out sheets.
- **Who to ask / owner:** Michael. Michael normally handles check-in of incoming deliveries (slide 12) and is the contact if phone certificate setup is blocked (slide 14: "Contact Michael if setup is blocked"). For questions about PATH, who checks packages in, or how it works, ask Michael.
- **Employees** only need to set up their badge and notifications, then pick up packages (slides 5 to 9) and optionally use the website (slides 10 and 11).
- **Sections:** Setting up (slides 5 to 7), Picking up packages (8, 9), Package website (10, 11), Checking in packages at the kiosk (12, 13) and by phone (14 to 16).
- **Network addresses:** the PATH website is on the internal network; the addresses are listed in slides 10 and 14 and may change.
